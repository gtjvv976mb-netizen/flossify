      2:00 Ready when you are

      The 2-minute brush

      Brush twice a day for two full minutes. Spend 30 seconds on each part of your mouth. We’ll tell you when to move on.

      1. Upper right
      2. Upper left
      3. Lower left
      4. Lower right

      Hold your brush at a 45° angle to the gumline and use gentle circles.
