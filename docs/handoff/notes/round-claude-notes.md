## A2 (round/book: 39bc8d8) — server half of the new "Blocked time (040)" section
> **Blocked time (040) — the server.** `clinic_unavailable(clinic, from, to)` is the one reader of closed time: the hours (`shut`), `clinic_hours.break_*` (`lunch`), a treating dentist's `staff_schedule` days and from/to (`hours`), and the dated `clinic_block` rows (`closed`, `leave`, `chair_out`), as dated ranges. `src/lib/blocks.ts` is the desk's side, always inside `withClinic`: `loadBlocks` (every range but shut, with names and notes; throws past 400 days), `findBlock` (the first range a visit sits in, in its scope — the clinic, its dentist, its chair — by priority closed, shut, lunch, leave, hours, chair_out), `addBlock` / `removeBlock` (under the book's lock `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext(clinic id))`, audit `schedule.block` / `schedule.unblock`), `closedIds`, `reopenNewlyClosed`, `visitsInClosedTime`, `keepInClosedTime`, `upcomingBlocks`, `readBlock` / `readWholeDays`, and `IN_SCOPE_SQL` for any SQL that asks whether a visit is in closed time. A block is only added or removed: the app has insert and `update (removed_at, removed_by)`, and a trigger refuses the rest.
> - **The soft stop.** `/api/schedule` POST, and PATCH when it moves (time, chair or dentist), run `findBlock` after `findClash`. In closed time the answer is 409 `{ error: <one sentence>, blocked: true, kind }`; `anyway: true` books it and sets `appointment.blocked_ok_at` (audit `appointment.anyway`), and a move out clears it. A walk-in (`status: 'arrived'`) is never asked — the patient is at the desk — and is marked the same way. A clash always wins: a hard 409 with today's sentence and no anyway. A status-only or words-only PATCH never looks at blocks. GET's Range adds `blocks` and `staff[].hours`; nothing else changed.
> - **`blocked_ok_at`** means someone saw the visit in closed time and kept it there (Book, Move or Place anyway, a walk-in, Calls → Keep it). Null means nobody has looked, and such a visit belongs on Calls → In closed time. A new block clears the mark on the visits inside it. A weekly change clears it only on visits it newly put in closed time (`closedIds` before, `reopenNewlyClosed` after, under the lock).
> - **`/api/schedule/blocks`**: POST `{ kind, dentistId?, chair?, startsAt, endsAt, note? }` → 201 `{ block, inside }`; PATCH `{ id, remove: true }` → 200 `{ removed }`. It uses the schedule's gate and limit, `X-CSRF`, and `schedule.edit`. `inside` lists the visits already booked there with their reminder's state; nothing is done to them, and no text is queued, cancelled or changed by a block (reminders still go, p07 §7.1).
> - **The words are `src/lib/block-words.ts`** (pure; the calendar imports it too): `blockSentence`, `blockLabel(r, 'chip' | 'strip', { day })`, `whyWords` (the Calls pill), `blockDone`. Whole Manila days are written as days; a block already under way says when it ends. `src/lib/reminder-state.ts` is the one wording of a visit's reminder (Calls, the Block panel). `src/lib/schedule-api.ts` holds the schedule API's gate, body readers and refusal (moved verbatim; a refusal may carry extra fields). `isDentistHere` is in `schedule.ts`.
- Layout: `src/lib/blocks.ts`, `block-words.ts` (blocked time over clinic_unavailable(), the words); `src/lib/schedule-api.ts`, `reminder-state.ts`; `src/pages/api/schedule/blocks.ts`.
- Notes for B3: import BlockRange with `import type` (blocks.ts imports pg); pass `{ day: ymd }` to blockLabel for strip labels. For A4: readWholeDays fields are kind/dentist/from/until/note; InsideVisit has reminderTone and reminderShort.

## C1 (round/pub: 7b72d9c) — under "Blocked time (040)", patients' side

A visit whose dentist is not listed here holds a chair but no listed dentist. Pick another."

Pick another day."); in a part of the day a closure shuts ("… closed then on …"); with a dentist chosen, on a day or part of the day that dentist is not in ("Dr. Cariño is not in on Tue 6 Oct. Pick another day, or any dentist."). Its placeholder never lands in lunch or in the dentist's time away. The "No word by" day skips days a closure shuts.
The status pill reads `closuresFor(l)`: the clinic's closures and lunch for 31 days, per Manila day, the same on a Find a clinic card and on the clinic's page. With none, every string is the pre-040 one. It dates the next opening only when that is more than a week away, or exactly a week because a closure moved it.
The hours card shows each day's lunch and "Closed days ahead" (the next 30 days, never a note). A dentist's days carry their own hours ("Here Tue 1–6 pm · Thu") on the clinic page, the booking's pick line and note, and `/dentists/<slug>`.
